# Dhruv Koshta Portfolio

The public portfolio for Dhruv Koshta: a fast, editorial, single-page portfolio with project case studies, writing links, and a small set of playful interactions.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The public site at `dhruvkoshta.me`, rebuilt from the current hand-drawn portfolio into the editorial system described in `docs/PHASES.md`.
_Avoid_: Website, personal site

**Section**:
A top-level content block on the home page with a stable anchor, such as `#projects` or `#skills`.
_Avoid_: Panel, block

**Index row**:
A dense list row used for projects, writing, experience, and competitions, with a title, dotted leader, optional metric, and date.
_Avoid_: List item, table row

**Polaroid**:
A captioned photo card used in the hero and "As a Kid" collages, with a small rotation and paper-like shadow.
_Avoid_: Photo card, thumbnail

**Receipt**:
The thermal-style contact interaction that renders a printable message and is sent from the contact section.
_Avoid_: Contact form, message box

**Radio**:
The persistent audio player for licensed background music.
_Avoid_: Music player, audio bar

**Pet**:
The small pixel-art companion that walks across the page and can be toggled off in settings.
_Avoid_: Mascot, cursor buddy

**Presence**:
The live visitor layer: facepile, visitor count, and optional live cursors.
_Avoid_: Analytics, tracking

**Phase**:
A shippable slice of the rebuild with its own scope, deliverables, and quality gate.
_Avoid_: Sprint, milestone

**Content status**:
Whether a section has real content (`ready`) or is waiting for Dhruv's data (`placeholder`).
_Avoid_: Draft, mock
